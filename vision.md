# Smart shopping

New Zealand being far away from anywhere in the world, and lack of manufactorying capabilities, a lot of goods are charged at a premium price due to distribution overhead (shipping, margin, storing cost etc...).  Although there are things like temu or aliexpress, the quality of products are often compromised. There should be great potential of reducing distrbution overhead.


## Ideas
To address above issues, I beleive a **Shopping research agent** could help find best buying options for certain product. it should do following:
- **Product analyze** search NZ providers for prices of different brand and specs
- **Substitution research** search for subsitutionary brands/products from main producing countries, and their price in NZD
- **Suggest buying options** based on price, shipping and needs of after sale service, suggest buying options
